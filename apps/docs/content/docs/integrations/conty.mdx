Conty is a Brazilian creator marketing platform for brands and agencies. It is the featured connector of the runelay catalog, at the top of **Popular**. Once it is connected, every agent that uses runelay can run the brand's creator campaigns through it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Auth** | OAuth |
| **MCP URL** | `https://mcp.appconty.com/mcp` |
| **Connection name** | `conty` |
| **Tool prefix** | `conty.` |

## What it does

The tools come from Conty's MCP server and act with the permissions of the Conty account that signed in.

- **Campaigns**: create a campaign from a brief (objective, deliveries per network, scopes, briefings), save it as a draft for review, edit, pause or reactivate it, renew deadlines, upload the cover and briefing files.
- **Creators**: search creators like the Creators tab of the Conty panel, resolve @handles, read a profile and its social numbers, Conty's AI analysis of a creator's content (brand fit, brand safety, what to avoid) and their history in Conty, keep creator lists.
- **Invites and applications**: invite one creator or hundreds in one call, approve or reject applications.
- **Content review**: list deliveries, approve videos with a rating and a posting date, request changes, reject, approve pending covers, captions and scripts in bulk.
- **Sales and coupons**: a coupon per creator, coupon banks, the commission budget, the coupon sales report and store orders from a connected Shopify or Nuvemshop.
- **Tracking**: tracking links and short links per creator, the campaign traffic funnel (click, visit, event, download) and the analytics connectors for PostHog, Google Analytics and App Store Connect.
- **Communities**: communities, channels, announcements, sharing campaigns with the community, community analytics and pending reviews.
- **Chat and notifications**: direct messages with creators and notifications.
- **Wallet**: balance, statement, creator payouts and PIX deposits. A deposit only generates the PIX charge; money moves when the brand pays it.
- **Brand overview**: videos, views and likes over time.

## Add it

1. Open **Connectors → Catalog** in the runelay panel.
2. Press **Connect** on the Conty card at the top of the catalog.
3. A new tab opens on Conty's sign-in page. Sign in with your Conty account, approve access and the tab closes on its own.

The connection is saved even if the sign-in tab does not open. Finish it later with **Finish sign-in** on the Conty card, or from the sign-ins queue at `/connectors/reconnect`. See [OAuth window not opening](/troubleshooting#oauth-window-not-opening).

Don't have a Conty account yet? Start at [appconty.com](https://appconty.com).

## Use it

Agents find Conty's tools with `search_tools` and run them with `call_tool` or `execute`. Tool names start with `conty.`. See [MCP tools](/mcp-tools).

Ask in plain language, for example:

- "Create a campaign for the X launch with 20 beauty creators on TikTok."
- "Approve the videos under review in campaign Y with a rating of 5."
- "How much did the creators' coupons sell this week?"

## Without the catalog

The catalog entry is a remote MCP connection to `https://mcp.appconty.com/mcp`. You can add the same URL as a [custom connection](/connections#remote-mcp) under another name.
